Engineering approach

How I work

Start from the physical system

Model assumptions are continuously tested against sensing, timing, actuator limits, contact behaviour, and hardware evidence.

Diagnose across layers

Robot failures rarely respect team boundaries, so analysis follows the signal across devices, networks, middleware, control, and task logic.

Design for repeatability

Clear tests, diagnostics, calibration procedures, and data workflows turn one successful demonstration into dependable capability.
