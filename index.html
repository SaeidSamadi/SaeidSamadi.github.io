Robotics from algorithm to deployment

Engineering dependable physical intelligence

I am a robotics engineer with more than nine years of experience across humanoid control, contact-rich manipulation, teleoperation, force and tactile sensing, and real-robot validation.

My work sits at the boundary between control theory and complex robotic systems. I develop whole-body and manipulation capabilities, integrate them with sensing and learned policies, and validate the complete stack in simulation and on hardware.

Across industry and research, I have worked with full-scale humanoids, TALOS, HRP-4, and dual-arm mobile manipulators. I enjoy the difficult systems problems where algorithms, production software, hardware, safety, and experimental evidence all need to agree.
