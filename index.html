About Me

As Robotics Lab Manager at the University of Edinburgh, I oversee 8 advanced labs, including the Fields Robotics Lab, Assistive Living Lab, Autonomous Vehicles Lab, Graphics Lab, and Bio-Inspired Lab. Blending innovation, research, and mentorship, I’m driven to create robots that seamlessly connect with humans and redefine possibilities in real-world environments.

My Background

With hands-on experience working with advanced humanoid robots like HRP-4 and Talos, I specialize in transforming challenges into breakthroughs. My expertise spans:

  • Real-time whole-body balance control in multi-contact scenarios,
  • Footstep planning and loco-manipulation for humanoids,
  • ROS-based software development for robotics platforms,
  • Navigation and control for quadruped and wheeled robots.
