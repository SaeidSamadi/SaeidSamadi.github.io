About Me

As Robotics Lab Manager at the University of Edinburgh, I oversee 8 cutting-edge labs, including the Fields Robotics Lab, Assistive Living Lab, and Autonomous Vehicles Lab. My role is a dynamic mix of 60% lab management, 30% research, and 10% teaching, allowing me to bridge innovation and mentorship.

With hands-on experience working with advanced humanoid robots like HRP-4 and Talos, I specialize in transforming challenges into breakthroughs. My expertise spans:

  • Real-time whole-body balance control in multi-contact scenarios
  • Footstep planning and loco-manipulation for humanoids
  • ROS-based software development for robotics platforms
  • Navigation and control for quadruped and wheeled robots

I am driven by the vision of creating robots that seamlessly interact with humans in dynamic, real-world environments, redefining what's possible in robotics.
