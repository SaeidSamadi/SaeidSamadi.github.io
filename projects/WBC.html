Case study · 2018–2022

Real-Time Whole-Body Balance Control

Control formulations that let HRP-4 remain stable while fixed, sliding, and moving contacts change during physical tasks.

  • HRP-4
  • Whole-body control
  • Quadratic programming
  • Force/torque sensing

Interchangeable fixed and sliding contacts

The central challenge was to control a humanoid through contact transitions without relying on a separately precomputed balance region. The framework unifies fixed and intentionally sliding contacts and computes a safe centre-of-mass position and wrench distribution online.

My contribution

  • Developed optimisation-based balance formulations around the Chebyshev centre.
  • Integrated contact constraints and force/torque feedback into the robot control pipeline.
  • Designed simulation studies and hardware experiments for wiping, shuffling, and non-coplanar support conditions.

Outcome

The resulting controller handled contact-mode transitions in real time and demonstrated stable behaviour on HRP-4 across challenging multi-contact tasks.

ICRA 2020

Mixed fixed and sliding multi-contact balance

This work introduced an analytically computed centre-of-mass support area for contact configurations that mix fixed and sliding supports. The region is used directly as a constraint in a centroidal quadratic programme for centre-of-mass and wrench optimisation.

  • Represented stability under intentionally sliding contacts.
  • Connected the geometric support region to online centroidal control.
  • Validated pushing and wiping behaviours on the HRP-4 humanoid.

ICAR 2021

Multiple fixed and moving unilateral contacts

The final strand improved the efficiency of geometric balance-region computation so it could be integrated directly into whole-body control. Dynamic region updates let HRP-4 adapt its centre of mass and contact forces while interacting with moving objects.
