Assistive Autonomy

Since Jan. 2025

The autonomous wheelchair and healthcare application aim to enhance the quality of life for individuals with mobility challenges. This project leverages advanced robotics and AI technologies to develop a wheelchair capable of autonomous navigation, obstacle avoidance, and user-friendly interaction. The system is designed to:

  • Navigate autonomously using SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping) and path optimization algorithms.
  • Provide voice and touch-based control interfaces for ease of use.
  • Integrate real-time people detection, activity monitoring, and fall detection to ensure safety.
  • Support NLP (Natural Language Processing) features for intuitive interaction with users.
  • Offer autonomous battery management for long-lasting and uninterrupted operation.

This innovative application also focuses on addressing loneliness and isolation for the elderly or patients through social interaction features, connecting users to healthcare professionals or loved ones. With modular software architecture, it can be easily integrated into existing robotic platforms and tailored to specific healthcare needs.

Initial testing will focus on elderly care homes, where the system can assist with daily tasks, ensure safety, and provide companionship. Future applications may extend to hospital settings, enabling improved patient mobility and caregiver efficiency.
