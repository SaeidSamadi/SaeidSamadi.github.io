Humanoids 2024

NAS: N-step computation of all footstep solutions

Challenge

A conventional footstep planner typically returns one solution for a particular objective. In changing environments, a robot benefits from knowing the complete feasible set so it can query or reselect plans rapidly as goals and constraints evolve.

Approach

NAS computes all feasible solutions to an N-step footstep-planning problem and constructs a globally optimal policy for efficient online queries. The implementation structures the computation for manageable bilinear complexity and GPU parallelisation.

Outcome

Simulation and TALOS experiments demonstrated minimum-step planning and responsive replanning across multiple locomotion scenarios.

IEEE T-RO 2024

Online multi-contact receding-horizon planning

Challenge

Long-horizon multi-contact planning captures difficult terrain and whole-body constraints but is too expensive to solve repeatedly at full fidelity. Short horizons are faster but can make poor local decisions without information about the future.

Approach

The work introduced two ways to approximate the missing long-term value:

  • Multi-Fidelity RHP uses accurate dynamics in the execution horizon and relaxed dynamics farther into the prediction horizon.
  • Locally Guided RHP uses a learned oracle to predict intermediate goals and build local value functions for efficient short-horizon planning.

Outcome

Extensive simulations and TALOS experiments showed feasible, responsive multi-contact planning across dynamic and uneven environments.
