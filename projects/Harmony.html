Harmony EU Horizon Project

Nov. 2018 - Feb. 2022

An Efficient Representation of Whole-Body Model Predictive Control for Online Compliant Dual-Arm Mobile Manipulation

This project introduces a novel bilevel Model Predictive Control (MPC) framework for dual-arm mobile manipulators, enabling efficient whole-body motion planning in dynamic and constrained environments. The first stage, MPC-T, computes long-horizon object-centric trajectories for the dual end-effectors, while the second stage, MPC-W, refines the task-space motion into short-horizon whole-body trajectories, incorporating compliance and safety constraints. The key innovation is the integration of Bezier curve parameterization, ensuring faster computation and smooth trajectory generation for high-DOF robots.

The project achieves:

  • Fast and adaptive object-oriented motion planning using Bezier curve-based representations.
  • Efficient generation of whole-body trajectories with stringent safety and compliance requirements.
  • Experimental validation in dynamic obstacle avoidance, narrow-space navigation, and coordinated dual-arm manipulation.
