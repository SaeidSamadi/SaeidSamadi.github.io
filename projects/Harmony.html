Bilevel planning for a high-DOF robot

Challenge

A dual-arm mobile manipulator must coordinate its base and both arms while respecting safety and compliance constraints. Solving the entire long-horizon, whole-body problem at once is computationally expensive—especially when obstacles and task requirements change online.

Approach

The framework separates planning into two connected model-predictive-control stages:

  • MPC-T computes long-horizon, object-centred trajectories for both end effectors.
  • MPC-W turns the task-space plan into short-horizon whole-body motion with compliance and safety constraints.
  • Bézier-curve parameterisation reduces the decision space while preserving smooth, adaptable trajectories.

Outcome

Real-robot experiments demonstrated coordinated manipulation, online obstacle avoidance, narrow-space navigation, and adaptive whole-body motion on the Harmony platform.
